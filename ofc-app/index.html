Scoring rules used

Each pair of players

  • Every player is compared with every other player, row by row. Win a row: +1. Lose: −1. Tie: 0.
  • Win all three rows (scoop): +3 bonus, so +6 total.
  • Royalties are settled as the difference: if you have 8 and your opponent has 2, you get +6 from them.
  • Totals are zero-sum.

Fouls

  • Rows must get stronger going down: bottom ≥ middle ≥ top. Otherwise the hand is fouled.
  • A fouled hand scores no royalties and is scooped by every clean hand (−6, plus that opponent's royalties).
  • Two fouled hands score 0 against each other.

Fantasyland

  • It doesn't change the points. A Fantasyland hand is scored like any other.
  • Getting in: QQ or better on top without fouling.
  • Staying in: trips on top, a full house or better in the middle, or quads or better on the bottom, without fouling.
  • Progressive: QQ = 14 cards, KK = 15, AA = 16, trips = 17. Staying in = 14.
